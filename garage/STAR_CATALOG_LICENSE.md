Changes: retained HIP ID, common name, RA (hours), declination (degrees) and
visual magnitude for stars of magnitude <= 3 plus vertices used by the four
line figures. Excluded the Sun and entries without HIP IDs. This derived
catalog remains under CC BY-SA 4.0. No endorsement by the catalog author.

The line figures are simple manually specified connections: Big Dipper
(an asterism in Ursa Major), Cassiopeia, Orion, and Cygnus. These are not
official IAU boundaries. Other code and assets retain their existing licenses.
